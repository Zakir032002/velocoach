# Voice and licence of the cue audio

The spoken cues in this folder are synthetic speech rendered from the phrase bank
(`crates/vc-core/src/phrase.rs`, printed by `vc-cli phrases`), with earcons generated by
`tools/make_earcons.py` (sine tones; no third-party audio).

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Engine | Piper (`piper-tts` Python package; version in `RENDERED_WITH.txt`). The maintained engine, OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl, is GPL-3.0; the engine is **not** vendored here, only its output |
| Voice | `en_US-ljspeech-medium` |
| Model card | https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/main/en/en_US/ljspeech/medium (MODEL_CARD) |
| Dataset | LJ Speech (Keith Ito): "All text, audio, and annotations are in the public domain" — https://keithito.com/LJ-Speech-Dataset/ |
| Why this voice | `en_US-lessac-*` is trained on Blizzard 2013 Lessac data licensed for non-commercial use only, and voices fine-tuned from lessac (amy, ryan, hfc_female, libritts_r) inherit that question; macOS `say` voices exclude public-sharing and commercial use. A demo shown to a company and published on video needs the public-domain option |
| Exact command | `scripts/render_cues.sh` (downloads the voice to `build/voices/`, renders to `build/phrases/`, mixes earcons into this folder) |

Not legal advice: LJ Speech is the lowest-risk choice found, not a guarantee.
